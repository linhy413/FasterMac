# Commercial licensing

The public components can be used commercially under AGPL-3.0-only. Charging
for software or operating a business does not by itself require payment to
the author. The applicable AGPL conditions and the Section 7 attribution terms
still apply.

If your intended integration cannot comply with those terms, you may request
a separate commercial agreement from [linhy413](https://github.com/linhy413)
through the [licensing issue form](https://github.com/linhy413/FasterMac/issues/new?title=Commercial%20licensing%20inquiry).
Describe the component, intended distribution and required rights; do not post
confidential contracts or credentials in a public issue.

An inquiry, quote request or issue is not a license. An alternative license
exists only after the relevant rights holder grants it in a separate written
agreement. It can cover only material for which that rights holder has the
necessary rights, including any applicable contributor grants.

The complete FasterMac application is a separate proprietary product. Its
license is not supplied by the public component license. Existing compliant
AGPL grants are not withdrawn by later commercial offerings.
